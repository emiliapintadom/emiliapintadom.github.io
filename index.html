The 2024 election if only one group had voted

Using the 2024 Cooperative Election Study (60,000 respondents), I estimated how white voters with and without a degree, $100K+ earners, Latino and Black voters voted in every state, and who would have won the Electoral College if only each group had voted. States where the lead is not significant or the sample is too small are flagged, not guessed.

Women in the Economy monitor: young women in Mexico City

I produced the data for the Mexico City section of IMCO's monitor: I processed INEGI's survey microdata in R and calculated the indicators on young women (15 to 29), for the city, its 16 boroughs and the country.

  • Labor participation, informality, entrepreneurs, NEET rate
  • Working hours and hours of unpaid housework
  • Every estimate with its standard error, checked against the previous year
